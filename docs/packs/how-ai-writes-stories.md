# How AI writes stories --- what to print

For the 60- and 90-minute workshops, in groups of 4-5. Everything here is A4
landscape. The 60-minute workshop needs only `instructions.pdf`,
`generation-ledgers.pdf` and `counters.pdf`: its training round is done
together, on the whiteboard.

## instructions.pdf

One page: how a ledger sheet works, the colours, the training round and the
generation round. Print one for yourself; the groups don't need it.

## generation-ledgers.pdf

The five pre-tallied picture-book models, one after another, five sheets each.
Give each group one book and hand out one sheet per person, so the group holds
the whole model between them. The page map at the bottom of this file says which
pages are which book.

Print double-sided (either binding works): each book is padded to an even page
count, so no leaf holds two groups' books, and the last page of a book may be
blank. Two members of a group then share a leaf, which is fine at one table ---
print single-sided if you'd rather every person had their own. With more than
five groups the books repeat, so spread the repeats around the room rather than
seating two groups with the same book side by side.

## training-ledgers.pdf

One blank ledger sheet: empty rows, four coloured boxes each, no words. Print
one per person --- five to a group --- for the 90-minute workshop's training
round, where the words and the marks are both theirs to write.

## training-texts.pdf

Six short texts as the tokeniser read them, one page each: five outdoors texts
(beach, storm, kookaburra, creek and bush) and an excerpt from _Fun with Dick
and Jane_ (the big blue ball). A solid box marks where a word starts a new
row, a dashed box one whose row is already on the sheet. Print one page per
group and give every group a different text: the finale pools them, and it only
works if no two groups hold the same model. The page map below says which page
is which text.

## training-texts-2up.pdf

The same six texts in the same order, typeset at A5 and printed two to an A4
sheet: print single-sided, then guillotine each sheet down the middle. Three
sheets give all six.

## counters.pdf

Coloured squares to cut up, if you have no counters. Print double-sided (either
binding works) --- one copy gives 24 of each colour.

## Also needed, not in this pack

- counters in four colours (red, blue, green, yellow), at least six of each per
  group, a paper cup and a tub per group
- butchers paper for the class story (90 minutes), pen and paper per group
- a projector, a whiteboard, and a chatbot for the opening
