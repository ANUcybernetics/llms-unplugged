How AI writes stories (ledger) --- what to print
================================================

A 90-minute workshop for groups of 4-5. Everything here is A4 landscape.
Print one set per group; with more than five groups the sets repeat, so
spread the repeats around the room rather than seating two groups with the
same book side by side.

The lesson entry, with timings and the rest of the materials list:
https://www.llmsunplugged.org/lessons/how-ai-writes-stories-ledger/


generation/  --- one book per group, already tallied
-----------------------------------------------------
Each folder holds:
  ledger.pdf     a facilitator brief, then five sheets. Print single-sided
                 and hand out one sheet per person.
  counters.pdf   coloured squares to cut up, if you have no counters.
                 Print double-sided (either binding) --- one page gives
                 eight of each colour.

Every set is capped at four followers a word, so every word sits on one row.
Four of the books are cut to their first 140 tokens; Green Eggs and Ham is
held at 100, because the slides walk through that set row by row.


training/  --- one text per group, tallies left to make
--------------------------------------------------------
Each folder holds:
  ledger.pdf     brief plus five sheets with the words printed and the
                 strips empty. One sheet per person.
  counters.pdf   as above.
  text.pdf       the text as the tokeniser read it, numbered token by
                 token. One copy per group, for whoever reads aloud.

Give every group a different text --- the finale pools them, and it only
works if no two groups hold the same model.


Also needed, not in this pack
------------------------------
- counters in eight colours (red, blue, green, yellow, pink, purple, black,
  white), at least eight of each per group, a bag and a tub per group
- butchers paper for the class story, pen and paper per group
- a projector, a whiteboard, and a chatbot for part 1

Rebuild this pack with `make pack-how-ai-writes-stories-ledger` in the
llms-unplugged repo.
