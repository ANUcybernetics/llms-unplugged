# My first language model --- what to print

For the 60-minute, 90-minute and 2-hour workshops, in pairs or small groups.
What each length needs:

- **60 minutes:** `grid.pdf` and `training-text.pdf`
- **90 minutes and 2 hours:** those two, plus the booklets

The 2-hour workshop's extra sections (tool calls, and scaling up) need nothing
printed: the tool calls are the participants' own text messages.

## grid.pdf

The blank 32-word grid participants train their model in. A3 landscape, one per
pair or group. Print it at A3: reduced to A4 the cells are too small to tally
in.

## training-text.pdf

The opening of _Green Eggs and Ham_, ready to tally. One per pair or group. Its
vocabulary fits the grid with a few rows to spare.

Better still, bring your own kids' books and give each pair a different one: a
page or two each is plenty, and different texts make the share-backs more fun.
Pick a short, repetitive page, because a grid only has room for 32 different
words.

## booklets/

Pre-trained models, bigram, to generate from with a d10. One per pair or group,
for the pre-trained generation round. They're A4 with recto and verso margins,
so print double-sided and staple down the left edge. Mix the texts around the
room: the share-back is better when neighbours are reading different models.

- `green-eggs-and-ham.pdf` and `the-cat-in-the-hat.pdf` (Dr Seuss, 5 and 7
  pages): short, quick to search, good for younger groups
- `the-man-from-snowy-river.pdf` (Paterson, 48 pages): the one the slides walk
  through
- `beatles.pdf` (36 pages)
- `a-christmas-carol.pdf` (Dickens, 55 pages)
- `frankenstein.pdf` (Shelley, 107 pages): the biggest; one or two copies for a
  keen pair

The Materials page has more, including trigram booklets and a Chinese one.

## Also needed, not in this pack

- a d10 per pair or group (any die works with a little improvisation)
- pens or pencils
- a projector for the slides
