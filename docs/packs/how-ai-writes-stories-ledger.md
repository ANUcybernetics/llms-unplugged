# How AI writes stories (ledger) --- what to print

A 90-minute workshop for groups of 4-5. Everything here is A4 landscape.

## instructions.pdf

One page: how a ledger sheet works, the colours, the training round and the
generation round. Print one for yourself; the groups don't need it.

## generation-ledgers.pdf

The five pre-tallied picture-book models, one after another, five sheets each.
Give each group one book and hand out one sheet per person, so the group holds
the whole model between them. The page map at the bottom of this file says which
pages are which book.

Print double-sided (either binding works): each book is padded to an even page
count, so no leaf holds two groups' books, and the last page of a book may be
blank. Two members of a group then share a leaf, which is fine at one table ---
print single-sided if you'd rather every person had their own. With more than
five groups the books repeat, so spread the repeats around the room rather than
seating two groups with the same book side by side.

## training-sheets.pdf

One blank sheet: empty rows, four coloured boxes each, no words. Print one per
person --- five to a group --- for the training round, where the words and the
marks are both theirs to write.

## training-texts.pdf

The five school-day texts as the tokeniser read them, numbered token by token,
one page each. Print one page per group and give every group a different text:
the finale pools them, and it only works if no two groups hold the same model.
The page map below says which page is which text.

## counters.pdf

Coloured squares to cut up, if you have no counters. Print double-sided (either
binding works) --- one copy gives 24 of each colour.

## Also needed, not in this pack

- counters in four colours (red, blue, green, yellow), at least ten of each per
  group, a paper cup and a tub per group
- butchers paper for the class story, pen and paper per group
- a projector, a whiteboard, and a chatbot for part 1
