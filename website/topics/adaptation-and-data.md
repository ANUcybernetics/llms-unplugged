# Adaptation and Data

![Hero image: Adaptation and Data](/assets/images/hero-adaptation-and-data.avif)

Discover how models are customised for specific tasks and the risks of training
on synthetic data.

## Lessons in this topic

### [LoRA](/lessons/lora)

Add a lightweight adaptation layer to retarget a trained model without
retraining everything.

### [Synthetic Data](/lessons/synthetic-data)

Generate synthetic text with your model, retrain on it, and see how patterns
drift or collapse.

## What you'll learn

After completing these lessons, you'll understand:

- How to efficiently adapt models to new domains
- Why fine-tuning is more practical than training from scratch
- The dangers of model collapse from synthetic data
- Why data quality and provenance matter for AI systems
