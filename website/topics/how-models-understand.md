# How Models "Understand"

![Hero image: How Models Understand](/assets/images/hero-how-models-understand.avif)

Explore how models use context and represent word meaning through embeddings.

## Lessons in this topic

### [Context Columns](/lessons/context-columns)

Add context columns to your bigram model to capture grammatical patterns, then

## What you'll learn

After completing these lessons, you'll understand:

- How attention mechanisms work at a conceptual level
- Why grammatical context improves text generation
- How words are represented as numbers (vectors)
- Why similar words have similar representations
- The mathematics behind word relationships
- How modern AI "understands" language semantically
