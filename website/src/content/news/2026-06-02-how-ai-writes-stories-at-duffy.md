Last Tuesday we took _LLMs Unplugged_ into a Year 5 classroom at
[Duffy Primary School](https://www.duffyps.act.edu.au/) (around 35 students) for
a lesson we called _How AI Writes Stories_. The session came about through the
[ACT Academy of Future Skills](https://canberradaily.com.au/new-future-skills-academy-for-north-canberra/),
who've been helping us get these materials in front of students and teachers
across the territory's public schools.

Most of our workshops so far have been with teachers, education professionals,
or high-school students. Duffy was our first session with upper-primary
students, and we weren't sure how they'd find the model-building activity.

![Duffy Primary Year 5 students seated facing the front of the room as two facilitators present a slide headed "Writing"](../../assets/images/duffy-workshop-01.avif)

We ran the [cutouts version of the activity](/decks/how-ai-writes-stories/):
students sorted word cards into buckets, then built new sentences by drawing
cutouts one at a time.

![A worksheet bordered with cut-out word tiles framing a passage of handwritten text the students generated from their model](../../assets/images/duffy-workshop-03.avif)

The students asked why the model sometimes produces nonsense, where the
"creativity" comes from, and what changes if you feed it a different book. Some
of their questions made us think, too.

![A student holding up their handwritten workshop notes, headed "Cybernetics"](../../assets/images/duffy-workshop-02.avif)

Thanks to Pip Hall and the Duffy Primary team for having us, and to the ACT
Academy of Future Skills for making the connection. There are already more
primary schools lining up. If you'd like to run something like this with your
students, do [get in touch](/about/#get-in-touch).
