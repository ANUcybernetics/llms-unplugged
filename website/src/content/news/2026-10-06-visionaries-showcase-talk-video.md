In August I gave a fifteen-minute talk at the ANU Visionaries Among Us Showcase,
to a hall of about 360 people. Partway through, ushers handed everyone a
[search sheet](/modules/search-sheets/). For four minutes the room ran a
language model by show of hands, generating a sentence one word at a time. ANU
TV has now put the video on YouTube.

I've recut the middle section so that a viewer can follow the run. While the
room works, the screen splits three ways: the stage, the sentence growing on the
board, and a grid of the sheets. The sheets holding each called-out word light
up as the hands go up.
