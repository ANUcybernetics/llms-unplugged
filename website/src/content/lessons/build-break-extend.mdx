A half-day deep dive using the paper cutout models. Participants build their own
language models, pool them into bigger ones, deliberately break them (training
in sycophancy), extend them with tools, and put everything together in a closing
poetry slam where each group designs a model to perform its own generated verse.

<Callout type="info">

We've delivered this lesson once and it worked, but it hasn't had the polish of
the others yet---timings and materials may shift as we road-test it. If you're
thinking of running it, [get in touch](/about/#get-in-touch) and we'll share
what we've learned.

</Callout>

## Run of show

| Section                            | Time   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------ |
| Icebreaker and intro               | 20 min |
| [Training](/modules/training/)     | 20 min |
| [Generation](/modules/generation/) | 20 min |
| Combining models                   | 25 min |
| _Break_                            | 10 min |
| [Sycophancy](/modules/sycophancy/) | 20 min |
| [Agentic AI](/modules/agentic-ai/) | 20 min |
| Poetry slam                        | 45 min |

Combining models and the poetry slam are deck-only sections (they don't have
standalone module pages yet): combining models pools two groups' cutout spreads
to show what more training data buys you, and the poetry slam has each group
design a model---choosing training data and sampling rules---to generate and
perform a poem.

## You will need

- printed token cutouts for several texts (bigram, `-n 2`)---see the
  [Materials](/tools/#cutouts) page for ready-to-print packs
- sycophancy cutouts for the "break" section: there's no ready-to-print pack
  yet, so generate them with the CLI
  (`llms_unplugged cutouts -i data/sycophancy.txt -n 2 --duplex`)
- clear table space per group of 2-4, plus room to pool spreads when groups
  combine models
- pen and paper per group
- a projector for the slides
