# The Fellowship of the Unplugged

For more than two decades,
[CS Unplugged](https://www.csunplugged.org) has shown that the core ideas of
computing can be taught without a computer --- with cards, string, chalk and a
lot of running around. The activities are used in classrooms, lecture theatres,
museums and living rooms, in dozens of languages, by people who have never met
the people who wrote them.

The Fellowship of the Unplugged is the group of people who look after that
work: keeping it available, keeping it current, and keeping the community that
uses it connected to each other.

## The charge of the Fellowship

- Keep the lore of Computer Science Unplugged preserved in the libraries of the
  internet, so that its activities, guiding principles, and purpose remain known
  to all Free Peoples.
- Maintain the fellowship of the Unplugged community, ensuring that friends and
  allies remain connected across distant lands.
- Bring news of newly forged activities to the attention of the realm, that they
  may be seen and shared.
- Renew the scrolls and records of the website as new technologies arise, lest
  the knowledge become as ancient as the ruins of Arnor.
- Welcome new companions to the quest, including scholars, apprentices,
  researchers, curriculum-makers, and other seekers of wisdom.
- Grant clear leave for others to use and share the work under the seal of the
  Creative Commons, for some folk desire written authority before they journey
  forth.

## Using the material

Yes, you can use it. You don't need to ask, and you don't need our permission
in writing --- but if your school, university or ministry needs it in writing,
this is it.

- [CS Unplugged](https://www.csunplugged.org) activities are released under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): use, adapt,
  translate and share them, including commercially, as long as you credit the
  source and release your version under the same terms.
- [Classic CS Unplugged](https://classic.csunplugged.org) is still under
  [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), which
  adds a non-commercial restriction.

## Two sites, one Fellowship

Unplugged is a method, not a fixed set of activities, and it keeps growing. The
work lives in two places, and they point at each other.

- [CS Unplugged](https://www.csunplugged.org) --- the original: algorithms, data
  representation, cryptography and computational thinking, without a computer.
  The Fellowship's charge lives there, and
  [csunplugged.org/fellowship](https://www.csunplugged.org/fellowship) is its
  canonical home.
- [LLMs Unplugged](https://www.llmsunplugged.org) --- the same method applied to
  AI: build a language model by hand with a children's book, a grid and some
  dice, and find out what ChatGPT is actually doing. Actively developed, and
  where its lessons, modules and printable materials are published.
