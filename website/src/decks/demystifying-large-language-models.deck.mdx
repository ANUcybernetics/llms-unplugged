the sentence we wrote is in **no book**

![bg](./assets/bg-chain.avif)

```notes
Nobody wrote it: there was no lookup in a big database of sentences, and nobody pulled out their phone and searched the web. Everything was decided by the token pairs on our collective sheets.

So where did they come from?
```

## three books

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<p class="corpus-a fragment">“He was an old man who fished alone in a skiff in the Gulf Stream.”<br /><span class="corpus-attribution">— Ernest Hemingway, <em>The Old Man and the Sea</em> (1952)</span></p>
<p class="corpus-c fragment">“TRUE! nervous, very, very dreadfully nervous I had been and am.”<br /><span class="corpus-attribution">— Edgar Allan Poe, <em>The Tell-Tale Heart</em> (1843)</span></p>
<p class="corpus-b fragment">“The sun did not shine. It was too wet to play.”<br /><span class="corpus-attribution">— Dr. Seuss, <em>The Cat in the Hat</em> (1957)</span></p>

```notes
Each token pair on the sheets came from one of these three books. Click them in one at a time and read each out.

The sheets (the model) had no indicator of which token came from which book. It worked because the books share tokens: `the`, `and`, and `.` are in all three, and the chain drifts from one book into another through them.
```
