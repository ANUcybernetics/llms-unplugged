## The challenge

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-design-challenge.avif)

design a poetry language model from scratch

write your recipe on a blank lesson card; another group will build it and
perform the result

your model card plus their performance is the completion task

---

## Design your model (20 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-design-your-model.avif)

what _isn't_ poetry?

what does _your_ model do, and why?

write up your model on a blank lesson card to give to another group

---

## Pre-slam prep (10 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-prep-rehearsal.avif)

swap lesson cards with another group

you have **10 minutes** to generate as much text as you can from their model,
then plan a one-minute performance
