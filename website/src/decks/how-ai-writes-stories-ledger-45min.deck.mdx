```comment
The 90-minute ledger deck cut to 45 minutes by dropping whole partials
(yr5-6-what-is-ai, yr5-6-pattern-warmup and ledger-bucket) and swapping
ledger-training for ledger-training-quick, where the whole room trains one row
("the") by ear instead of groups tallying a new text. Five parts:
  Part 1 (4 min)   Words (yr5-6-definitions --- form groups of 4-5 before this)
  Part 2 (5 min)   Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 3 (20 min)  Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 4 (6 min)   Where did the marks come from? (ledger-training-quick)
  Part 5 (5 min)   Wrap-up + Q&A (yr5-6-wrap-up)

Two partials point back at dropped beats: the definitions notes tie "AI" to the
story a chatbot wrote, and the wrap-up suggests re-running the Part 1 chatbot
story. Skip those tie-backs.

Before each delivery: print the pre-tallied picture-book sets, the counters
page and the instruction sheet from the 90-minute deck's pack (see that deck's
comment); the blank sheets, the outdoors text pages and the butchers paper
aren't needed. A whiteboard and a helper to tally for part 4.
```
