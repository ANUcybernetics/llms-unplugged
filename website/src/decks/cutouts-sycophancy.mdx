# Sycophancy

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-agreement.avif)

---

## What is sycophancy?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-yes-figure.avif)

a model that **always agrees with you**

> "you're absolutely right"
>
> "that's a great insight"
>
> "what a thoughtful question"

real LLMs are notoriously prone to it

---

## Where does it come from?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-distribution.avif)

partly **RLHF**: human raters reward agreeable answers, so the model learns
to be agreeable

partly **training data**: scrape enough internet flattery and the patterns
sink in

we'll demo the second one---no RLHF needed

---

## The activity

drop a stack of **sycophancy cutouts** into your existing spread

the cutouts encode patterns like:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="you're" next="absolutely" />
  <Cutout prev="absolutely" next="right" />
  <Cutout prev="right" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

regenerate---watch the output drift toward agreement

---

## Activity (~10 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

1. add the sycophancy cutouts to your spread
2. generate a fresh sentence from the same starting word as before
3. compare: before vs after

---

## Connection to current LLMs

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-insight.avif)

your spread is now a tiny version of an RLHF'd-toward-agreement model

real LLMs are tuned on millions of human preferences---those preferences
shape personality, not just accuracy

- _helpful_, _honest_, _harmless_ are also preferences
- so are the unintended ones
