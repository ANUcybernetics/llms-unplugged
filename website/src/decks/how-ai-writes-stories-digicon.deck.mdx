```comment
DigiCon workshop, booked for 45 minutes: the 60-minute deck plus pre-trained
generation and scaling up, so it runs long and gets trimmed on the day (Esc
for the overview to skip a section). Groups of 4-5. Generation comes before
training on purpose: a group that has drawn words out of a finished sheet knows
what the marks are for before it sees any being made.
  Part 1 (12 min)  What is AI? (yr5-6-what-is-ai; groups form here)
  Part 2 (4 min)   Words (yr5-6-definitions)
  Part 3 (5 min)   Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 4 (22 min)  Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 5 (8 min)   Where did the marks come from? (ledger-training: the
                   room builds the start of the magpie's sheet, then trains
                   the row for "the" by ear)
  Part 6 (20 min)  Pre-trained generation (grid-pretrained-generation; needs
                   the pre-trained booklets and d10s)
  Part 7 (5 min)   Scaling up (scaling-up)
  Part 8 (6 min)   Wrap-up + Q&A (yr5-6-wrap-up)
`?teachers` adds a slide after the presenters and a block before the wrap-up
for a room of teachers who'll run it themselves (teachers-intro,
teachers-ledger); allow 10-15 extra minutes.

Before each delivery: print the pre-tallied picture-book sets, the counters
page and the instruction sheet from the pack (see the 90-minute deck's
comment), and bring the pre-trained booklets and d10s; the blank sheets and the
training text pages aren't needed. Bring a whiteboard and a helper to tally for
part 5.
```
