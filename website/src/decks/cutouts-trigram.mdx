## Why two words of context?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-core-insight.avif)

bigram models ask: _"what follows this word?"_

trigram models ask: _"what follows these two words?"_

predictions get sharper, but the spread gets bigger and sparser

---

## The algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-algorithm-training.avif)

same as before---each cutout now has a **two-word prefix** (two coloured
boxes) followed by the token

cut, spread, generate

## Example spread

---

## Generation

match the **rightmost** prefix box to your most recent word, then check the
box to its left matches the word before that

---

## When you get stuck: data sparsity

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-randomness.avif)

if no cutout matches both of your prefix words, fall back to matching just the
rightmost box---you've effectively dropped to a bigram for that step

this is the **data sparsity problem**: more context = sharper predictions =
exponentially more training data needed

---

## Activity (~20 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-resources.avif)

swap your bigram cutouts for the trigram cutouts of the same text

generate a sentence---does it feel more natural than the bigram output?
