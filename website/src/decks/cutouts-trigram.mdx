# Trigrams

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-paper-mechanism.avif)

---

## Why two words of context?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-insight.avif)

bigram models ask: _"what follows this word?"_

trigram models ask: _"what follows these two words?"_

predictions get sharper, but the spread gets bigger and sparser

---

## The algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-cut-spread.avif)

same as before---each cutout now has **two previous words** (two coloured
boxes) followed by its next word

cut, spread, generate

---

## Example spread

<LmCutouts tokens={GREEN_EGGS_TOKENS} ngram={3} columns={2} />

most two-word combinations are unique---each one only points to one or two
next words

---

## Generation

match the **rightmost** previous-word box to your most recent word, then
check the box to its left matches the word before that

---

## Activity (~20 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

swap your bigram cutouts for the trigram cutouts of the same text

generate a sentence---does it feel more natural than the bigram output?
