## The _language_ of language models

![bg right:60%](./assets/bg-language-of-lms.avif)

- open weight model

{/* notes:
Everyday sense first, then ours: the weights are the numbers inside the model --- for the booklet, the tallies you'd count to work out what comes next --- and *open weight* means anyone can have a copy of those numbers. The booklet is the clearest version of that we'll get: take it home, read every number in it, generate from it at the kitchen table, and no-one can switch it off or quietly change it under you. Most of the chatbots they use are the other arrangement --- your text goes to someone else's computer, an answer comes back, and the weights never leave the building. If anyone pushes: open weight isn't open source (you get the numbers, not the training text or the code that made them), and it isn't the same as being able to run it --- a real open weight model wants a serious machine, where the booklet wants a d10.
*/}
