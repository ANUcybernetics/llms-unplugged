# Shareback

![bg](./assets/bg-div-reception.avif)

{/* notes:
Take a couple of groups' generated sentences; celebrate the weird ones. ~5 min.

If the room has energy for it, close the shareback by turning it outward: does that change how you picture what's happening when you type into ChatGPT? Then wait --- the silence is longer than feels comfortable and the first answer usually arrives just after you'd have given up. If nothing comes, narrow it: "did anything about that feel like something you've seen it do?"

Two habits worth keeping. Don't answer your own question, and when someone raises something technical, consider flipping it back before you take it: "what draws you to that?" or "has anyone else run into that?" The expertise in the room is more use to them than a lecture from the front.
*/}

## The _language_ of language models

![bg cover left:60%](./assets/bg-shannon.avif)

- prompt
- completion
- hallucination

{/* notes:
Everyday sense first, then ours: to prompt is to nudge an actor who's forgotten a line --- here the prompt is the starting text you give the model. A completion is finishing something off --- here it's the text the model generates back, and it doesn't know when to stop. A hallucination is seeing something that isn't there, a malfunction --- but the sentence you just generated was never in the text either, and it came out of the same tallies and the same dice as everything else. There's no separate mode the model slips into; we only call it hallucination when we didn't want the answer.
*/}
