## The challenge

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-design-challenge.avif)

design a poetry language model from scratch

write your algorithm (recipe, or procedure) down on paper; another group will build it and
perform the result

your model card plus their performance is the completion task

{/* notes:
They design a poetry model on paper; another group builds and performs it. No single right answer --- that's the point.
*/}

---

## Design your model (20 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-design-your-model.avif)

**in pairs**

what _isn't_ poetry?

what does _your_ model do, and why?

write up your model on a blank lesson card to give to another group

{/* notes:
Hands off --- this one's theirs. Nudge "what isn't poetry?" if a group stalls. 20 min, circulate.
*/}

---

## Pre-slam prep (10 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-prep-rehearsal.avif)

swap lesson cards with another group

you have **10 minutes** to generate as much text as you can from their model,
then plan a one-minute performance

{/* notes:
Swap cards; 10 min to generate from another group's model and plan a one-minute performance.
*/}

## The _language_ of language models

![bg right:60%](./assets/bg-vocabulary.avif)

system prompt

temperature

evaluation

prompt engineering

{/* notes:
Gloss each: system prompt = standing instructions that set the model's behaviour; temperature = how random the next-word choice is; evaluation = how you judge whether output is any good; prompt engineering = wording the input to get what you want.
*/}
