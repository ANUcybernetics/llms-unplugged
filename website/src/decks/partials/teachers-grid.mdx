## Running it in your class

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

pairs, each with a grid template, a d10 and a few pages of a kids' book

**60 minutes** trains and generates; **90** adds the pre-trained booklets; **2
hours** adds tool use

pairs that finish early can train on a second page, or move on to the Sampling
module

```notes
Step out of the student role here, and say so. Then the logistics, briefly, and hand over to their questions --- they will have them.

The snags you saw today are the ones their class will hit: a token only gets a new row and column the first time it appears, quote marks are noise, and the full stop is followed by the next word, page break or not. On the dice: sharing ten faces across a row's tallies is the step students need modelled most; if a class isn't comfortable with it, run the Weighted Randomness module first.
```
