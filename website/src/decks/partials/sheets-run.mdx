## the algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-algorithm-generation.avif)

1. I **call out** the last token we wrote (and its colour)
2. **hands up** if it's the _boxed_ token on your page
3. I **pick a hand at random** --- you read out the token beside it
4. I **write it down**, and we go again

```notes
Walk it once, slowly.

Step 2: one hand each. More than one match? Go with the first one you spot.

Step 3: I pick at random. Taking the fastest hand would sample the quickest reader in the room rather than the text.

For the seed: someone near the front reads out any one pair from their sheet, boxed word first. Pulling it off a sheet means the room supplies the first two words as well as every word after them. Take whatever pair they give you, even a full stop or a word that appears once. It can't dead-end.
```

```notes
Shift-W (or click the board and type), write the volunteer's two words, call out the second of them, go. Aim for 12--15 tokens, written BIG for the back of the room. Keep the rounds moving.

Narrate two things while it runs. First, the hands are the distribution: call a common word and a forest goes up; call a rare one and you get two. The number of hands for a continuation is how often it happens in the text, so the room is doing weighted sampling without being taught it. (If someone asks: a person holding several matches for one context raises one hand, which flattens the busiest words slightly.) Second, the choice is a coin toss: when five hands are up, the four I don't pick were all real continuations. Pick a different hand, get a different sentence, which is why the "regenerate" button gives a different answer.

Every distinct token appears as a boxed word somewhere, so hands will go up every round unless a sheet went home with an empty seat. If the room goes quiet, say that's why and reseed from the brief on page 1 (avoid restarting from a full stop).

Stop around 15 tokens and read the sentence back.
```

## what role did you play?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-core-insight-portrait.avif)

each word on your sheet → a **token**

the pairs on your page → the **parameters** of the model

the hands in the air → the **probability distribution** over next tokens

my random pick → **sampling**

the sentence I wrote → **generation**

```notes

(If it comes up: real models chop rarer words into sub-word pieces so they can spell words they haven't seen.) Parameters and weights are two names for the same thing.
