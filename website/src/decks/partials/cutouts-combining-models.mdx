## Your goal

## Worked example: two piles, one spread

you trained on _Green Eggs and Ham_; they trained on _Peter Rabbit_---two
different models, each with their own characteristic cutouts:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
  <Cutout prev="the" next="rabbit" />
  <Cutout prev="rabbit" next="ran" />
  <Cutout prev="ran" next="away" />
</CutoutRow>

shared words like <Token next>the</Token>, <Token next>i</Token>,
<Token next>.</Token> bridge between the two---your chain can drift from one
text into the other mid-sentence:

> alone: _"i am sam . sam i am ."_
>
> combined: _"i am sam . the rabbit ran away ."_

same mechanism, just a richer pile pulling in two directions

{/* notes:
Shared words (the, i, .) bridge the two books, so the chain can drift from one into the other mid-sentence. That drift is the whole point.
*/}

## Activity (15 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-activity-generation.avif)

combine both spreads on one table

generate 1--2 sentences from the merged pile

notice: does it sound like one book, the other, or a mash-up?

{/* notes:
Generate 1--2 sentences from the merged pile, then have them judge whose book it sounds like. 15 min, circulate.
*/}
