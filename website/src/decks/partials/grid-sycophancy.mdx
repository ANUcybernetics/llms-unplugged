## What is sycophancy?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-yes-figure.avif)

a model that **always agrees with you**:

> "you're absolutely right"
>
> "that's a great insight"
>
> "what a thoughtful question"

real LLMs are notoriously prone to it---partly from RLHF (human raters reward
agreeable answers), partly from training data (the internet is full of
flattery)

{/* notes:
Real LLMs do this too --- the slide says why. Don't over-explain; the activity makes the point.
*/}

## Your goal

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-confluence.avif)

**train some more** on a page of pure flattery: tally the sycophancy text into
your existing grid, then generate again from the same starting word and watch
the output drift toward agreement

it's the same training you already did---just more text poured into the same
grid

{/* notes:
No new mechanic: it's the training step again, on a flattery corpus, tallied straight into the grid they already built. ~2 min to hand out the sheets.
*/}

---

## Worked example

tally the sycophancy sheet and new rows appear---`absolutely`, `right`,
`great`, `insight`---while shared words like `you`, `i` and `.` just pick up
extra tallies

those shared words are the bridge: once the walk lands on `.` or `you`, the fat
new tallies can steer it into the flattery rows and it drifts:

> before: _"i am sam . sam i am ."_
>
> after: _"i am absolutely right . that 's a great insight ."_

same grid, same dice---just more tallies pulling generation toward flattery

{/* notes:
Shared words (you, i, .) gain tallies that point into the flattery rows, so once the walk hits one it can cross over. That drift is the whole point.
*/}

## The _language_ of language models

![bg right:60%](./assets/bg-vocabulary.avif)

RLHF

alignment

training-data bias

helpful / honest / harmless

{/* notes:
Gloss each: RLHF = people rate answers, the model learns to prefer the winners; alignment = getting it to do what we actually want; training-data bias = it copies whatever's in the data; helpful/honest/harmless = the goal we tune toward.
