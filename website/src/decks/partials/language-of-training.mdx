## The _language_ of language models

![bg right:60%](./assets/bg-markov.avif)

- model
- token
- weights

```notes
Everyday sense first, then ours: a model is a fashion model or a model aeroplane --- here it's the thing that captures the patterns, the tallies on paper in front of them. A token is a bus token or a token gesture --- here it's one word or punctuation mark. Weights are what you lift at the gym, or what you weigh up before deciding --- here they're just the tally marks, the numbers that make some next words likelier than others. Point at a tally: when you hear a model has billions of weights, that's what's being counted.
```
