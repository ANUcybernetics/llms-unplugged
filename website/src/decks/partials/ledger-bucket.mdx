## Five texts, five models

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-five-texts.avif)

each group trained on a different text, so each group's row for `the` is different

together the class has a **bigger model** than any one group

```notes
Ask each group what came after "the" in their text. Nobody has "volcano" except the volcano group; nobody has "bus" except the bus group. The class as a whole knows all of them.
```

---

## The bucket

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-bucket.avif)

I'll call out the word we're on

if your sheets have a row for it, throw in **one ball of your group's colour for every mark on that row**

I draw one ball --- that group draws from **their own bag** to get the next word

```notes
Two draws per word: the bucket picks the group, the group's bag picks the word. Groups without the row throw nothing. A group whose text used the word a lot throws more balls and gets picked more --- the more your text said it, the more say you get.
```
