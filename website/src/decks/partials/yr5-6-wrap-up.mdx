Let the class answer these; none of them has a single right answer:
- the words came from the book, the choices came from the cup, and the group chose where to start and stop, so whose story is it?
- each group's model only has the words from its book, and a chatbot only has what was in its training data: what might it have missed or read too much of, and how would you tell?
- back to the story from the start: their paper model doesn't know what a magpie is, it only picks a likely next word. A chatbot does the same thing at a much larger scale, so does it know what it's saying? Leave that question open.
