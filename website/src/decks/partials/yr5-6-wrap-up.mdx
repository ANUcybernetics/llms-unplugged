```notes
Take answers rather than supplying them; none of these has one right answer. First: the words came from the book, the choices from chance, and the group decided where to start and stop --- so whose story is it? This is the argument about AI and authorship, from something they just did. Second: their models only have their book's words, so a chatbot only has what it read --- what did it never read, or read far too much of, and how would you notice? Third, back to the story from the start: their paper model doesn't know what a magpie (or any word in its book) is, it picks a likely next word. The chatbot does the same at a vast scale, so does it know what it's saying? Leave that one open.
```
