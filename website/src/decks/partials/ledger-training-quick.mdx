# Where did the marks come from?

![bg](./assets/bg-tally-training.avif)

```notes
The sheets were finished when they arrived. Now the reveal: somebody made every mark by reading the text. We'll make one row together, all at once.
```

---

## Read it two words at a time

<LedgerTraining tokens={MAGPIE_OPENING} step={1} palette={PALETTE} />

```notes
This is the start of The magpie. Read it two words at a time: find the row for the first word, put a mark beside the second, then move along by one. A new word after the same word takes the next empty box, so a row's colours are the order the text met its followers.
```

---

## Listen for "the"

<LedgerRow entry={ROW_THE} palette={PALETTE} showTallies={false} />

every time you hear **the**, shout the word that comes **next**

```notes
Read The magpie aloud, slowly, leaning on every "the" (19 of them, counting the capital "The"s). The room shouts the next word; a helper tallies it on the whiteboard in four boxes --- magpie, fence, postie, dog, in that order. About 90 seconds.

The magpie is back. It sits on the fence. It watches. Here comes the postie. Down comes the magpie. Swoop! The postie runs, and up it goes, and back to the fence. It sits on the fence. It watches. Here comes the dog. Down comes the magpie. Swoop! The dog runs, and up it goes, and back to the fence. It sits on the fence. It watches. Here comes the postie. Here comes the dog. Down comes the magpie. Swoop! The postie runs, and the dog runs, and up it goes, and back to the fence. It is spring, and the magpie is back.
```

## Check the sheet

<LedgerRow entry={ROW_THE} palette={PALETTE} id="quick-the" />

the same marks as the magpie group's sheet: **5, 6, 4, 4**

a computer did this for **every word** in **every book**

```notes
Compare the whiteboard with the row: magpie 5, fence 6, postie 4, dog 4. Every row on every group's sheets was made exactly this way; the only difference is that a computer read the books. Real AI does the same thing with a huge amount of text.
```
