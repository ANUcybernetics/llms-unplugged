## Train your model

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-cut-spread.avif)

with your group you'll need **printed cutouts**, **scissors**, and a **clear
table**

cut out the cutouts for your text and spread them on the table---each cutout
pairs a **next word** with its **previous word**:

<Cutout prev="green" next="eggs" />

that's it---the spread _is_ your trained **language model**

{/* notes:
