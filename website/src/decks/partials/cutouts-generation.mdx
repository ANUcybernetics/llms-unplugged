## Your goal

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-generation-chain.avif)

generate new text by chaining cutouts together---write a word, find a cutout
whose previous-word box matches, write its next word, then hunt for the next
match:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="will" next="eat" />
  <Cutout prev="eat" next="them" />
  <Cutout prev="them" next="here" />
</CutoutRow>

the chain grows (like dominoes)

{/* notes:
A worked "find the next cutout" on the same pile from the overview, with the
running output ("your page") threaded above it. The page tail ("...and") fixes
the word we're matching. Four auto-animate frames (id "hunt") narrow the pile:
everything lit, then "and"'s colour (a fast scan --- note "there" shares that
colour), then the real "and" cutouts (there→. drops out), then one pick --- whose
next word ("ham") lands on the page in gold. Drives home that colour is a quick
filter but you confirm the word, and that each pick grows your text. The page
chain (will eat them here and ham) is the same one the worked-example table
builds out two slides on --- and it's a recombination, not a quote: the book runs
"...here and there", but the model rolls "and ham" instead, mashing "eat them
here and" together with "and ham". Worth saying aloud --- it's the "hint, not a
copy" payoff on the impact slide. Adjust page/target/pick or the seed to vary
the demo.
*/}
