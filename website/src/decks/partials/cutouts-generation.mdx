<CutoutsOverview
  tokens={OVERVIEW_TOKENS}
  mode="hunt"
  seed={4}
  page="will eat them here and"
  target="and"
  stage="pick"
  pick="ham"
/>

```notes
The generation loop runs as one auto-animate sequence (id "hunt") on the
seeded overview pile, with the running output ("your page") growing above it. It
opens on the full pile, then dims everything except the chosen starting cutout
(will eat), which gives the first two words before the hunt begins. From there
we follow the chain four picks deep: each pick's next word becomes the word we
match next, so the page builds will eat → them → here → and → ham. Each step
lights the whole pile, narrows by colour, and picks one cutout whose next word
lands on the page in gold to seed the next step. Where two words share a colour,
an extra exact-word frame sits between the colour scan and the pick ("them"
shares a colour with "do", and "and" shares with "there", whereas colour alone
isolates "eat" and "here"). Colour is a quick filter; confirm by the word when
two words share a colour. The finished chain "will eat them here and ham"
recombines two parts of the book: the original runs "...here and there", while
the model rolls "and ham" after "eat them here and". Adjust the
pages/targets/picks or the seed to vary the demo.
```

```notes
Groups chain words from their spread; the more text the better. 10 min, circulate and unstick anyone who can't find a match.
```
