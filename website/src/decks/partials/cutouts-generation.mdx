# Generation

![bg](./assets/bg-chain.avif)

## Your goal

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-generation-chain.avif)

generate new text by chaining cutouts together---write a word, find a cutout
whose previous-word box matches, write its next word, then hunt for the next
match:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

the chain grows (like dominoes)

---

## Worked example: pick a starting word

let's start with <Token next>i</Token>---scan the spread:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />
</CutoutRow>

three cutouts match---but two of them are <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />, so
your eye lands on <Token next>am</Token> about ⅔ of the time---no dice, no
probability tables, the spread is doing the maths

(two unrelated words can share a colour---verify the word, not just the colour)

---
