## Your goal

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-generation-chain.avif)

generate new text by chaining cutouts together---write a word, find a cutout
whose previous-word box matches, write its next word, then hunt for the next
match:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

the chain grows (like dominoes)

---

## Worked example: chain it together

<div class="worked-example-grid"><div class="we-header"></div><div class="we-header">cutout you pick</div><div class="we-header">your page</div><div class="we-step">start</div><Cutout prev="i" next="am" /><div class="we-page"><strong>i am</strong></div><div class="we-step">match <Token next>am</Token></div><Cutout prev="am" next="sam" /><div class="we-page">i am <strong>sam</strong></div><div class="we-step">match <Token next>sam</Token></div><Cutout prev="sam" next="." /><div class="we-page">i am sam <strong>.</strong></div><div class="we-step">match <Token next>.</Token></div><Cutout prev="." next="i" /><div class="we-page">i am sam . <strong>i</strong></div><div class="we-step">match <Token next>i</Token></div><Cutout prev="i" next="do" /><div class="we-page">i am sam . i <strong>do</strong></div></div>

---

{/* _class: impact */}

_"i am sam . i do..."_

a hint of the original, not a copy

---

## You will need

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-generation-supplies.avif)

**with your group**

your trained cutouts spread (already on the table)

pen and paper to write down the generated text

---

## Activity

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-activity-generation.avif)

generate as much text as you can from your spread---keep chaining words until
you run out of matches (or time!)
