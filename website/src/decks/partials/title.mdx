# LLMs Unplugged

## Understand AI by building it yourself

![bg](./assets/bg-title-wide.avif)

```notes
Open with the promise: understand AI by building a language model by hand, with no computers or maths background needed.

---

**Four facilitation habits for the session:**

**Don't apologise for explaining things.** Opening with "sorry, this bit's a little dry" or "bear with me" tells the room the next few minutes are a chore. The explanations are why they came.

**Don't call anything "the fun part".** Singling one section out as fun implies the rest is a slog, whether you're tallying, rolling the dice, or debating AGI at the end. Just introduce what comes next.

**Turn questions back to the room before answering.** A clarifying question ("what draws you to that?") or opening it to the floor ("has anyone else hit this?") keeps people participating, and you can still add your own answer after they've weighed in.

**Let them do the activity, then ask what they noticed.** When you reach a reflection slide, leave silence for the room to answer rather than supplying the takeaway yourself. A participant saying "the training text shapes what comes out" lands much better than you saying it.
```
