## What is this about?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-core-insight-portrait.avif)

you'll **build** your own language model---from scratch---with just a kids book,
pen & paper, and some dice rolling

you'll **learn** how language models work by spotting patterns in text to
generate new text
