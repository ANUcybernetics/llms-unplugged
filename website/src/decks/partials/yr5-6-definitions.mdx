<p class="fragment"><strong>artificial intelligence (AI)</strong> — technology that mimics some human abilities, like writing stories or answering questions</p>
<p class="fragment"><strong>language model</strong> — a system that predicts what word comes next, using patterns it learned from text</p>
<p class="fragment"><strong>training</strong> — building a language model by finding patterns in text (its <em>training data</em>)</p>
<p class="fragment"><strong>generation</strong> — using a language model to write new text, one word at a time</p>

```notes
Four words we'll lean on all workshop --- reveal them one at a time and tie each back to what they've already seen: AI wrote our story; a language model predicted the next word; we'll train ours by spotting patterns in the kids' book (our training data), then use it to generate a story of our own.
```
