```comment
The 60-minute deck with two parts added after its training walkthrough: every
group tallies its own outdoors text onto blank sheets, then the class pools
those models into one story.
  Preamble (6 min)  title, country, presenters, icebreaker, about
  Part 1 (6 min)    Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 2 (22 min)   Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 3 (8 min)    Where did the marks come from? (ledger-training)
  Part 4 (15 min)   Training on a new text (ledger-training-groups)
  Part 5 (10 min)   One story, all together (ledger-bucket --- drop if running
                    over; groups call out their row, the draws happen at the
                    front)
  Part 6 (6 min)    Scaling up (scaling-up)
  Part 7 (10 min)   Questions (denouement)

Every set is built so that no word has more than four followers, and the room's
palette is those same four colours, so every row is one row of red, blue, green
and yellow --- there are no continuation rows and no second palette to explain.

Before each delivery:
- print the pack: https://pdf.llmsunplugged.org/packs/how-ai-writes-stories-ledger.zip
  --- five PDFs (the five pre-tallied picture books for the generation round,
  one blank sheet for training, the five numbered text pages, the counters page,
  and the one-page instruction sheet) with a README saying what to print and
  which pages go to which group. Rebuild it with
  `make pack-how-ai-writes-stories-ledger` in the repo if the sets change and
  re-upload; the Makefile holds the exact commands, so don't rebuild them by
  hand.
- counters in four colours (red, blue, green, yellow), at least 10 of each per
  group (the largest tally in any set), one paper cup and one tub per group
- butchers paper for the class story
- a whiteboard and a helper to tally for part 3
```
