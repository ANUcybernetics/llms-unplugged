```comment
60-minute workshop in groups of 4-5. Generation comes before training on
purpose: a group that has drawn words out of a finished sheet knows what the
marks are for before it sees any being made. The 90-minute deck is this one
with the groups' own training and the class story added.
  Preamble (6 min)  title, country, presenters, icebreaker, about
  Part 1 (6 min)    Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 2 (22 min)   Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 3 (8 min)    Where did the marks come from? (ledger-training --- the
                    room builds the start of the magpie's sheet, then trains
                    the row for "the" by ear)
  Part 4 (6 min)    Scaling up (scaling-up)
  Part 5 (10 min)   Questions (denouement)

Before each delivery: print the pre-tallied picture-book sets, the counters
page and the instruction sheet from the pack (see the 90-minute deck's
comment); the blank sheets and the outdoors text pages aren't needed. A
whiteboard and a helper to tally for part 3.
```
