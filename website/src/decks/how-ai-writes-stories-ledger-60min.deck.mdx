```comment
60-minute workshop for students aged 10-16, in groups of 4-5. Generation comes
before training on purpose: a group that has drawn words out of a finished
sheet knows what the marks are for before it sees any being made. The
90-minute deck is this one with a warm-up, each group training on its own
text, and the class story added.
  Part 1 (12 min)  What is AI? (yr5-6-what-is-ai --- groups form here)
  Part 2 (4 min)   Words (yr5-6-definitions)
  Part 3 (5 min)   Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 4 (22 min)  Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 5 (8 min)   Where did the marks come from? (ledger-training --- the
                   room builds the start of the magpie's sheet, then trains
                   the row for "the" by ear)
  Part 6 (6 min)   Wrap-up + Q&A (yr5-6-wrap-up)
`?teachers` adds a slide after the presenters and a block before the wrap-up
for a room of teachers who'll run it themselves (teachers-intro,
teachers-ledger); allow 10-15 extra minutes.

Before each delivery: print the pre-tallied picture-book sets, the counters
page and the instruction sheet from the pack (see the 90-minute deck's
comment); the blank sheets and the outdoors text pages aren't needed. A
whiteboard and a helper to tally for part 5.
```
