## the algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-algorithm-generation.avif)

1. I **call out** the last token we wrote (and its colour)
2. **hands up** if it's the _boxed_ token on your page
3. I **pick a hand at random** --- you read out the token beside it
4. I **write it down**, and we go again

{/* notes:
- get the seed HERE, write it on the NEXT slide (ink is per-slide): someone near the front reads any pair, both tokens, boxed one first --- the room supplies the beginning too
- walk the four steps once against those two words
- labour step 3 only: at random, not whoever shouts first --- that samples the fastest reader, not the text. Random is what makes the room behave like the model
*/}

## what role did you play?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-core-insight-portrait.avif)

the pairs on your page → the **parameters** of the model

the hands in the air → the **probability distribution** over next tokens

my random pick → **sampling**

the sentence I wrote → **generation**

{/* notes:
- brisk: four words that made this sound unreachable, and the room now has a physical referent for each
- one sentence on training, closing the hole in the mapping: I counted every pair in the source text, in my laptop, before you arrived --- that counting is all "training" is
- land: a trillion parameters means a very large pile of the thing on your lap
*/}
