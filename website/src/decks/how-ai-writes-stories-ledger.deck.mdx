```comment
Every group generates from a different picture book (see the lesson entry for
   the five sets), and the slides walk through one of them: every ROW_* below
   is a real row of the Green Eggs and Ham set, the first 100 tokens dealt
   across 5 sheets, printed for a room with balls in eight colours ---
   `llms_unplugged ledger -i data/green-eggs-and-ham.txt --max-tokens 100
   --sheets 5 --palette @cli/ledger-palette-eight.json --prefill tallies`,
   whose palette is the first eight of the CLI's default
   (cli/ledger-palette.json), which is what PALETTE below slices. The firstRow
   passed to each widget is the row's physical position on its sheet, which is
   what decides its colours, so a regenerated set (a different --max-tokens, a
   different sheet count) means re-reading the rows and positions off the new
   sheets. No Green Eggs prefix runs past one row at 100 tokens, so the
   two-row slide borrows ROW_A_BEAR from the Bear Hunt set (same command,
   data/were-going-on-a-bear-hunt.txt). The bucket groups are the school-day
   texts' rows for "the" (data/school-day-*.txt), each built with --prefill
   prefixes for training.
```

```comment
90-minute workshop for students aged 10-16, in groups of 4-5. Generation comes
before training on purpose: a group that has drawn words out of a finished
sheet knows what the marks are for before it makes any. Seven parts:
  Part 1 (12 min)  What is AI? (yr5-6-what-is-ai)
  Part 2 (8 min)   Warm-up + words (yr5-6-pattern-warmup + yr5-6-definitions)
  Part 3 (5 min)   Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 4 (20 min)  Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 5 (25 min)  Training on a new text, then a short generation (ledger-training)
  Part 6 (10 min)  One story, all together (ledger-bucket --- drop if running over)
  Part 7 (5 min)   Wrap-up + Q&A (yr5-6-wrap-up)
Allow 5 min for arrival/settle/introductions before Part 1.

Before each delivery:
- print the pre-tallied picture-book sets (5 sheets each, plus the brief), a
  different book per group; print the school-day sets with --prefill
  prefixes, a different text per group, with their text.pdf pages
- soft balls in the palette's eight colours, at least 8 of each per group
  (the largest tally in any set), one bag and one tub per group, one bucket
  for the room
- a few "predict the next word" prompts for the whiteboard
```
