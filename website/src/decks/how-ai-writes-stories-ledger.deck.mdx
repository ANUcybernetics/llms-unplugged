```comment
Every group generates from a different picture book (see the lesson entry for
   the five sets), and the slides walk through one of them: every ROW_* below
   is a real row of the Fun with Dick and Jane set, the first 140 tokens dealt
   across 5 sheets, printed for a room with counters in four colours ---
   `llms_unplugged ledger -i data/fun-with-dick-and-jane.txt --max-tokens 140
   --sheets 5 --palette @cli/ledger-palette-four.json --max-followers 4
   --prefill tallies`, which is the recipe the Makefile runs for every book.
   Four colours at four columns is a single palette cycle, so every row on
   every sheet takes the same four and no widget here needs to know where its
   row sits on the page. Change the book or the budget and the counts below
   have to be re-read off the new sheets. SHEET_THREE is that set's third
   sheet entire, in the order the deal printed it. The five ROW_THE_* are the
   school-day texts' rows for "the" (data/originals/school-day-*.txt), each
   set built with --prefill prefixes for training.
```

```comment
90-minute workshop for students aged 10-16, in groups of 4-5. Generation comes
before training on purpose: a group that has drawn words out of a finished
sheet knows what the marks are for before it makes any. Seven parts:
  Part 1 (12 min)  What is AI? (yr5-6-what-is-ai --- groups form here)
  Part 2 (8 min)   Warm-up + words (yr5-6-pattern-warmup + yr5-6-definitions)
  Part 3 (5 min)   Read the sheet (ledger-read)
  Part 4 (20 min)  Generation + shareback (ledger-generation)
  Part 5 (22 min)  Training on a new text, then a short generation (ledger-training)
  Part 6 (10 min)  One story, all together (ledger-bucket --- drop if running over)
  Part 7 (5 min)   Wrap-up + Q&A (yr5-6-wrap-up)
Allow 5 min for arrival/settle/introductions before Part 1.

Every set is built so that no word has more than four followers, and the room's
palette is those same four colours, so every row is one row of red, blue, green
and yellow --- there are no continuation rows and no second palette to explain.

Before each delivery:
- run `make pack-how-ai-writes-stories-ledger` in the repo. It builds every
  set this workshop needs --- the five pre-tallied picture books for the
  generation round and the five school-day training sets, each with its
  counters page and the training texts with their numbered text pages --- and
  zips them into out/packs/ with a README saying what to print. The Makefile
  holds the exact commands; don't rebuild them by hand.
- counters in four colours (red, blue, green, yellow), at least 6 of each per
  group (the largest tally in any set), one paper cup and one tub per group
- butchers paper for the class story
- a few "predict the next word" prompts for the whiteboard
```
