# Combining models

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-confluence.avif)

---

## What if two models met?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-models-meet.avif)

each pair of students has trained on a different text

what happens if we **pool the cutouts** into one big spread?

---

## What to expect

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-distribution.avif)

- words that were a previous word in both texts now have **more options** for
  the next word
- styles cross-pollinate---Dr Seuss meets Frankenstein
- common words (`the`, `and`, `.`) act as bridges between the two styles

---

## Activity (~10 min)

1. find a pair with a different trained text
2. pool both spreads onto one table (or join the whole-room pile, if your
   facilitator prefers)
3. generate a sentence using the combined spread
4. note where the styles "cross over"
