# Agentic tool use

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-tool-use-banner.avif)

---

## What makes a model an "agent"?

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-insight-tool-use.avif)

an agent is a model that can **call tools**: pause generation, get information
from outside, and continue

the loop: generate → trigger word → call tool → write the result → keep going

---

## Your goal

add **tool-use cutouts** to your spread and designate a tool operator

when generation hits a trigger word, pause---the operator runs the tool and the
result becomes the next word(s)

---

## You will need

your trained cutouts spread (already on the table)

a stack of tool-use cutouts to add in

a designated **tool operator** (a person or small group)

pen and paper to write down the generated text

---

## Tools: VOTE and ACTION

| Trigger                         | Tool            | Returns                       |
| ------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------- |
| <Token next tool>VOTE</Token>   | the room        | yes / no (majority)           |
| <Token next tool>ACTION</Token> | a chosen person | a word for what they just did |

---

## Worked example

new cutouts route generation into and out of the triggers:

**into**: <Cutout prev="we" next="ACTION" tool="next" /> <Cutout prev="the" next="VOTE" tool="next" />

**out**: <Cutout prev="ACTION" next="." tool="prev" /> <Cutout prev="VOTE" next="." tool="prev" />

generating from <Token next>we</Token>, you might pick <Cutout prev="we" next="ACTION" tool="next" />---write <Token next tool>ACTION</Token>

hand off to the tool operator---they pick someone, who does something and
reports back ("waved")---write <Token next>waved</Token> and continue

---

## Activity (15 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-activity-tool-use.avif)

add tool-use cutouts to your spread

generate---when you hit a trigger word, hand off to the tool operator

write the operator's answer down as the next word(s), and continue generating
