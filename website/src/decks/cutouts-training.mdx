## The algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-cut-spread.avif)

cut out your printed tokens, spread them face-up on the table

that's it---the spread _is_ your trained bigram language model

---

## Each cutout shows...

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-token-anatomy.avif)

a **next word** alongside its **previous word**---the word that came before
it in the training text

every word has its own colour, so the boxed and free-standing forms of the
same word match

---

## Example

_"I am Sam. Sam I am. I do not like green eggs and ham."_

after preprocessing:

`i` `am` `sam` `.` `sam` `i` `am` `.` `i` `do` `not` `like` `green` `eggs`
`and` `ham` `.`

---

## The trained spread

<LmCutouts tokens={GREEN_EGGS_TOKENS} columns={2} />

cutout on the table

---

## The proportionality trick

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-distribution.avif)

if spot follows see 40% of the time in the training text, then **40% of the
cutouts** with see as the previous word will have spot as the next word

whichever matching cutout your eye lands on, you've sampled in proportion to
the original distribution---no dice, no probability tables, the spread is
doing the maths

---

## Activity (~25 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

- cut out the bigram tokens for your assigned text
- spread them face-up on the table
- (optional) sort into piles by previous word once the loose-on-table flow
  makes sense
