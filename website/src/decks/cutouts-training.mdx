## The algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-cut-spread.avif)

cut out your printed cutouts, spread them face-up on the table

that's it---the spread _is_ your trained bigram language model

---

## Each cutout shows...

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-token-anatomy.avif)

a **next word** alongside its **previous word**---the word that came before
it in the training text:

<Cutout prev="green" next="eggs" />

every word has its own colour, so the boxed and free-standing forms of the
same word match

---

## Activity (~15 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

- cut out the bigram cutouts for your assigned text
- spread them face-up on the table
- (optional) sort into piles by previous word once the loose-on-table flow
  makes sense
