# Training

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-paper-mechanism.avif)

---

## Your goal

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-cut-spread.avif)

cut your cutouts and spread them face-up on a table

that's it---the spread _is_ your trained bigram language model

---

## You will need

- printed token cutouts for your assigned text
- scissors
- a clear table or flat surface

---

## Each cutout shows...

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-token-anatomy.avif)

a **next word** alongside its **previous word**---the word that came before it
in the training text:

<Cutout prev="green" next="eggs" />

every word has its own colour, so the boxed and free-standing forms of the same
word match

---

## Worked example

original text:

_"I am Sam. Sam I am. I do not like green eggs and ham."_

after preprocessing (lowercase, punctuation as separate tokens):

## Worked example: the spread

after cutting and spreading, the table holds every adjacent (previous, next)
pair from the text:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />
</CutoutRow>

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="." />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="i" />
</CutoutRow>

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="." next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="." next="i" />
  <Cutout prev="do" next="not" />
  <Cutout prev="not" next="like" />
</CutoutRow>

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="like" next="green" />
  <Cutout prev="green" next="eggs" />
  <Cutout prev="eggs" next="and" />
  <Cutout prev="and" next="ham" />
  <Cutout prev="ham" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

16 cutouts---<Cutout prev="i" next="am" /> appears twice because `i am` appears
twice in the training text

---

## Activity (15 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-group-activity.avif)

- cut out the bigram cutouts for your assigned text
- spread them face-up on the table
- (optional) sort into piles by previous word once the loose-on-table flow makes
  sense
