# Generation

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-chain.avif)

---

## Your goal

generate new text by chaining cutouts together---write a word, find a cutout
whose previous-word box matches, write its next word, then hunt for the next
match:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

the chain grows like dominoes

---

## You will need

- your trained cutouts spread (already on the table)
- pen and paper to write down the generated text

---

## Step by step

1. **pick a starting word**---any word that appears as a previous word
2. **find candidates**---scan for cutouts whose previous word matches your
   current word (colour first, then verify the word)
3. **pick one visually**---your eye lands on common next words more often
4. **write the next word down**---then put the cutout back in the spread
5. **repeat** with the word you just wrote as your current word

---

## The proportionality trick

after <Token next>i</Token> in our training text, <Token next>am</Token> follows
twice and <Token next>do</Token> follows once---so two of the three cutouts with
<Token next>i</Token> as the previous word point to <Token next>am</Token>:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />
</CutoutRow>

whichever matching cutout your eye lands on, you've sampled in proportion to the
original distribution---no dice, no probability tables, the spread is doing the
maths

## Worked example

start with <Token next>i</Token>---three cutouts match (<Cutout prev="i" next="am" />, <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />, <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />)---pick <Token next>am</Token>

now <Token next>am</Token>---two cutouts match (<Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />, <Cutout prev="am" next="." />)---pick <Token next>sam</Token>

---

## Worked example (cont.)

now <Token next>sam</Token>---two cutouts match (<Cutout prev="sam" next="." />, <Cutout prev="sam" next="i" />)---pick <Token next>.</Token>

now <Token next>.</Token>---two cutouts match (<Cutout prev="." next="sam" />, <Cutout prev="." next="i" />)---pick <Token next>i</Token>

generated so far: _"i am sam . i..."_---a hint of the original, not a copy

## A small caveat

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-insight.avif)

two unrelated words can occasionally share a colour

always **verify the word itself** before committing, not just the colour
