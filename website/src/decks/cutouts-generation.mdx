## The algorithm

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-algorithm-generation.avif)

write a word, find a cutout whose prefix matches, write that cutout's token,
hunt for the next match

it grows like a chain of dominoes

---

## Step by step

1. **pick a starting prefix**---any word that appears as a prefix
2. **find candidates**---scan for cutouts whose prefix matches your current
   word (colour first, then verify the word)
3. **pick one visually**---your eye lands on common tokens more often
4. **write the token down**---then put the cutout back in the spread
5. **repeat** with the new word as the prefix

---

## Worked example

<LmCutouts tokens={GREEN_EGGS_TOKENS} />

start with `i`---two cutouts match (one `am`, one `do`)---pick `am`

now `am`---two cutouts match---pick `sam`

now `sam`---two cutouts match---pick `.`

now `.`---two cutouts match (`sam`, `i`)---pick `i`

generated so far: _"i am sam . i..."_---a hint of the original, not a copy

---

## A small caveat

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-core-insight.avif)

two unrelated tokens can occasionally share a colour

always **verify the word itself** before committing, not just the colour

---
