# Generation

![bg brightness:0.5](./assets/bg-chain.avif)

---

## Your goal

generate new text by chaining cutouts together---write a word, find a cutout
whose previous-word box matches, write its next word, then hunt for the next
match:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

the chain grows like dominoes

---

## You will need

- your trained cutouts spread (already on the table)
- pen and paper to write down the generated text

---

## Worked example: pick a starting word

let's start with <Token next>i</Token>---scan the spread for cutouts whose
previous-word box matches:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />
  <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />
</CutoutRow>

three cutouts match---but two of them are <Cutout prev="i" next="am" />, so your
eye lands on <Token next>am</Token> about ⅔ of the time---no dice, no
probability tables, the spread is doing the maths

pick a cutout, write down its next word, put the cutout back in the spread

---

## Worked example: continue the chain

we wrote <Token next>am</Token>---scan for <Token next>am</Token> matches:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />
  <Cutout prev="am" next="." />
</CutoutRow>

pick <Cutout prev="am" next="sam" />---write <Token next>sam</Token>

now <Token next>sam</Token>---two matches:

<CutoutRow>
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />
  <Cutout prev="sam" next="i" />
</CutoutRow>

pick <Cutout prev="sam" next="." />---write <Token next>.</Token>

(verify the word itself before committing---two unrelated words can occasionally
share a colour)

---

## Worked example: keep going

from <Token next>.</Token>, two cutouts match (<Cutout prev="." next="sam" />, <Cutout prev="." next="i" />)---pick <Token next>i</Token>

back to <Token next>i</Token>---this time your eye lands on <Cutout prev="i" next="do" />---write <Token next>do</Token>

generated so far: _"i am sam . i do..."_---a hint of the original, not a copy

---

## Activity (15 min)

![bg right:40%](./assets/bg-activity-generation.avif)

generate 1--2 sentences from your spread

read the funniest one to the room
