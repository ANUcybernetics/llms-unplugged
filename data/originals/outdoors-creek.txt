title: The creek
author: LLMs Unplugged

The creek is low. The rocks are dry and the mud is warm. A yabby sits under a rock. A boot comes down and the yabby goes back under. Then it rains, and then the creek is high. The rocks are gone and the mud is gone. The yabby sits under a rock.
