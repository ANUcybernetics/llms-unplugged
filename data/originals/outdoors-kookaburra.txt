author: LLMs Unplugged

The kookaburra sits in the gum tree and it laughs, and nobody knows why. Then morning comes and the kookaburra laughs again. The cat looks up. The kookaburra looks down. The cat goes inside. Then a second kookaburra comes, so they both laugh, and then the street is awake.
