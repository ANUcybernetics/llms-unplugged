title: The storm
author: LLMs Unplugged

The sky goes dark. Then wind comes up and leaves go everywhere. Then rain comes down on the tin roof, and it is loud. The dog hides under a bed. It is very dark. Then the rain stops, the sky is clear, and then frogs start up. The dog stays under a bed.
