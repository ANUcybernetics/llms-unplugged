---
title: The Cat and the Moon
author: LLMs Unplugged
url: https://www.llmsunplugged.org
---

stars gleam.
bells ring and the sea hums softly.
bells echo over the night.
birds sing a song to the moon.
birds wake and morning comes.
stars fall over the cat.
stars shine and rain falls and wind blows.
waves rise and clouds drift across the sun.
waves sing to the night.
the moon sings a tune.
the cat dreams of a dream.
the dog sleeps and shadows dance under the sea.
the sun rises over the dog.
