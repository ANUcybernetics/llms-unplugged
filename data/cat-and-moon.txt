title: The Cat and the Moon
author: LLMs Unplugged

---

the cat dreams of a song.
the dog sleeps and birds sing to the moon.
the night falls and the sea sings a song to the sun.
the sun rises over the sea.
the moon sings to the dog.
birds sing a song to the night.
stars fall over the cat.
