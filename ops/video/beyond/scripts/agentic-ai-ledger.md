# Agentic AI: ledger

**Key idea (draft):** a tool call is a token the model draws like any other
word; something outside the model (the harness) pauses, runs the tool and
splices the result back in.

**Plays:** the ledger Agentic AI section (TASK-168), start of the section.

**Builds on:** the ledger Agentic AI deck section, which doesn't exist yet
(TASK-168); its recipe follows `partials/grid-agentic-ai.mdx`, so this script
mirrors `agentic-ai-grid` on the magpie's real ledger. Settle the "Builds on"
run once that deck lands. The walk is ". here comes the dog", then the row for
_dog_ (two red for full stop, two blue for _runs_): red comes out, a tool call.
The friend's reply ("and it wants my sandwich") is invented.

## Beat sheet

1. hook: agents are in the news, and they sound like a new kind of technology.
   It's the loop you already know, plus one rule
2. the rule: draw from the cup as before; any punctuation you draw is a tool
   call
3. **beyond:** every box holding punctuation lights across the sheets: every
   row's share of punctuation counters is its chance of calling the tool
4. the walk: ". here comes the dog", fill the cup, red: full stop: stop
5. the tool: text three friends "What comes next?" with the sentence so far
6. the result: write down the first reply whole, then the punctuation you drew,
   and carry on from the punctuation (no row for the friend's last word; there's
   always one for a full stop)
7. latency: leave a gap and back-fill
8. you were the harness
9. take the person out and the loop runs by itself
10. ending: in a few minutes, your group will be the harness

## Script

**USHINI (TC):** This is the one that's all over the news. AI agents: models
that browse the web, write code, book flights. It sounds like a whole new kind
of technology. It isn't. It's the loop you've already run, with one rule added.

**BEN (VO):** Here's the rule. Fill the cup and draw, exactly as before. But the
moment you draw a punctuation mark, a full stop, a comma, stop. That's a tool
call.

_Visual: the magpie's ledger sheets fanned on the desk, the cup and the paper
strip beside them. Every box holding a full stop, a comma or an exclamation mark
lights gold, sheet by sheet._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. generation is the same as before; 2. punctuation means
stop; 3. that stop is a tool call.

**USHINI (VO):** So the model doesn't decide to use a tool. It draws one, like
any other word. Some rows almost never call it. Some always do.

_Visual: the gold boxes brighten by their row's share: the rows for "fence",
"watches" and "runs" light fully, their only box punctuation; "magpie" and "dog"
light partway; "the" and "it" have no gold box at all._

_Beyond:_ the slides state the rule; this shows that "deciding" to call a tool
is the same draw as picking a word, which is the demystifying claim, and it's
how real models do it (a special token they learned to produce, sitting in the
numbers like any other).

_Reads (draft):_ 1. a tool call is in the counts like any other word; 2. some
rows call it more than others.

**BEN (VO):** Say you've written "here comes the dog". The row for "dog": two
red for a full stop, two blue for "runs". Draw one. Red. Full stop. Stop.

_Visual: the strip reads "here comes the dog"; the row for "dog" lights and four
counters drop into the cup. A red one comes out, the full stop box lights gold,
the pencil stops, and a phone slides into frame._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. an ordinary draw; 2. it lands on punctuation; 3. generation
pauses.

**USHINI (VO):** The tool is a text message. Send "What comes next?", and the
sentence so far, to three friends or group chats.

_Visual: the phone, top-down: a message types itself, What comes next? "here
comes the dog…", and copies slide up to three contact rows. A beat of nothing.
One reply bubble drops in: and it wants my sandwich._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the tool is a text message; 2. it sends the sentence so
far; 3. it goes to three people at once.

**BEN (VO):** The first reply back is your tool result. Write the whole thing
down, then the full stop you drew. Then carry on, from the full stop.

_Visual: the reply's words slide off the phone onto the strip after "here comes
the dog", the full stop follows them, and the row for full stop lights; its
counters fill the cup, one comes out, and the pencil moves again._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the first reply is written down whole; 2. then the full stop
you drew; 3. generation carries on from the full stop.

**USHINI:** Why from the full stop, and not from "sandwich"? Because nobody has
a row for "sandwich". But there's always a row for a full stop.

_Visual: the sheets' row words flick past, and the gap where "sandwich" would be
lights empty. Cut to the row for full stop, present and lit._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the sheets have no row for the friend's word; 2. the full
stop always has one, so continue from there.

**BEN:** Replies take time. If nothing's back by your next punctuation mark,
leave a gap and fill it in later.

_Visual: the strip: a bracketed gap left after a full stop, the pencil carrying
on past it; later a reply's words slide into the gap._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. you don't wait for a slow reply; 2. leave a gap, keep going,
fill it in later.

**USHINI:** Here's the bit that matters. The model never learned what comes next
after "the dog". It only drew when to ask. And the thing that paused it, sent
the message, and spliced the answer back in? That was you. You were the harness.

_Visual: the loop draws on in one stroke, gold arrowheads: draw, punctuation,
pause, message out, reply in, carry on. A flat person icon at its centre; as
each stage is named its arrow lights, and the icon's hand is on every one._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. the model only drew when to ask; 2. a person did every step
of the hand-off; 3. that person is the harness.

**BEN:** Take the person out, and you've got a model acting on its own. That's
what the news means by an agent. So: what would you let it do without asking you
first?

_Visual: the person icon fades; the loop keeps running on its own, arrows
lighting in turn._

_Reads (draft):_ 1. without the person, the loop runs by itself; 2. that's what
the news means by an agent.

**USHINI (TC):** Don't answer yet. In a few minutes, your group will be the
harness.
